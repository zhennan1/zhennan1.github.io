Hello, I'm Zhennan Wan. I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University in June 2026 and am continuing at Tsinghua toward an M.Eng. in Electronic Information (Computer Science and Technology), expected in June 2028.

My research focuses on post-training and test-time scaling for large language models (LLMs), with the goal of improving model and agent capabilities in complex settings such as long-context reasoning, long-form generation, coding, and multi-agent collaboration.

I am a co-first author of [Scaling External Knowledge Input Beyond Context Windows of LLMs via Multi-Agent Collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21471), accepted to the ACL 2026 Main Conference. The project code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/THUNLP-MT/ExtAgents). I have also worked on LLM coding capabilities at ByteDance TRAE and CodeLLM applications at Tencent.

More details are available in my [CV](/cv/).

You can reach me at [zhennan018@gmail.com](mailto:zhennan018@gmail.com) and find my projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/zhennan1).
